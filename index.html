Claw Catch

Steer the claw with your bare hand. Grab a floating ball and drop it in the bucket.

  1. Move your hand to move the claw. Up lifts it, down lowers it.
  2. Close your fist when a ball sits between the fingers.
  3. Open your hand above the bucket to let it go.

Sit about an arm’s length from the camera in good light. The mouse mode works like the hand: hold the button or Space to close.
